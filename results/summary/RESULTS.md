ECE/Brier = calibration (lower better). mAP = mean average precision of concept prediction. **CKA** = linear centered-kernel alignment of the representation z with the concepts (Fig. 4, higher = better). **Disentangle** = DCI disentanglement (Fig. 4, higher = better). **OIS** = Oracle Impurity Score and **NIS** = Niche Impurity Score (CIBM, arXiv:2602.14626; ×100, lower = purer concepts). **AUC-TTI / NAUC-TTI** = test-time-intervention curve area (lowest-confidence policy), intervenable models only. '--' = not applicable (e.g. no concept head) or not yet computed.
