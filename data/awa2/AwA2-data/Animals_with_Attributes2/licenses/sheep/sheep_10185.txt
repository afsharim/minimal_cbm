Dinnertime for the Sheep


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
About a week and a half ago, we drove out to Western Mass to visit <a href="http://www.hettiebellefarm.com/" rel="nofollow">Hettie Belle Farm</a>, which is where our Thanksgiving turkey came from, as well as much of the meat we eat during the year (we have a meat share from them, where we pay a fixed amount up front and then get a share of the meat as they slaughter animals over the next 5 months). This is one of the sheep eating its dinner of hay. You can see all the pictures I took in <a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/sackton/sets/72157628683048365/">this set.</a>
