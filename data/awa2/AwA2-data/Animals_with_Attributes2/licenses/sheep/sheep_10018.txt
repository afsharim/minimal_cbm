30 days of 85mm


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
(12 of 30) we went to a community of farm shops today and amongst the shops were animals including these sheep, who where a nightmare to take pictures of because no matter what I did they would not look at me!! Until that is I discovered that all responded to fart noises. So I proceeded to make a series of very loud fart noses. 

Whats the silliest noise you've had to make to distract an unwilling subject?
