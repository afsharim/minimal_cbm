We don't get humans much around these parts..  Far from the nearest town, near the western cliffs of the south island of New Zealand.  Sheep far outnumber people on NZ, and some of them look like they haven't seen a person in years.  To get to the cliffs, I had to walk through their pasture, careful of the lumps of.. uh.. fertilizer.
