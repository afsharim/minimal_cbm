We follow you everywhere


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Brad was released from the sheep hospital (the barn). He follows me everywhere now and tries to get in the house all the time. On the picture you can see him with Kleines. Both come OUT of my sleeping room. You can see Bach in the background. He's wondering if the sheep sleep there as well. After I explained to the disappointed sheep, that I do not always carry food with me, we went together to the sweet chestnut trees, where I gave them a sweet chestnut and told them to do the rest on their own. Everytime there is a problem, they behave like babies.. err lambs again.
