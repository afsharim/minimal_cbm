Cotswold sheep are docile and friendly. They have thick heavy fleeces and are capable of enduring hardship and exposure. It originated in the Cotswold hills of Great Britain, and was brought to Canada from England in the 1860s. It is recorded in 1865,  Cotswold sheep were purchased in Halifax at prices from $22 for a ram to $60 for ewes. 

Even though the wool of Cotswolds was very sought after, their continued main purpose was for both meat and mutton.
