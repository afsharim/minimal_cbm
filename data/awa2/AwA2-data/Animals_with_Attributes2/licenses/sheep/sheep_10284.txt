GOC Cheddington 032: Sheep


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Totally cute sheep (<i>Ovis aries</i>) at Long Marston, Tring Rural, Hertfordshire.

Hertfordshire GOC's 12 October 2013 walk, which was an 11-mile circular walk from Cheddington railway station in Buckinghamshire, visiting the villages of Long Marsden and Marsworth, mostly following the Grand Union Canal, including the Aylesbury arm. Please check out the other photos from the walk <a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/anemoneprojectors/sets/72157637040420755">here</a>, or to see my collections, go here: <a href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/anemoneprojectors/collections/">www.flickr.com/photos/anemoneprojectors/collections/</a>.
