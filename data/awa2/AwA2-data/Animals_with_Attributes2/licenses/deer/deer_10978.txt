Key Deer


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
A key deer photographed on Big Pine Key. These deer are a subspecies of the eastern white-tailed deer. Over the years they adapted to the tropics. They aren't much larger than a large dog. Although it's an extremely rare sight, some people have reported seeing small herds of them swimming from key to key.
