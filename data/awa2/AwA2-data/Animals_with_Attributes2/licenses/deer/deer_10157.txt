Deer + Droplets


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
<i>:: Sorting through my archives of images never posted ... </i>

Black tailed deer standing in the rain at China Camp, Marin County (April 2010)

She was grazing and looked back at me as I shot the photo. I was worried that I startled her and that she'd run off, interrupting her feeding. But, she went back to her business after I snapped the image. She probably just thought we were fools for hiking out there on that drenched day.
