Fallow Deer


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Later the same day a roe doe bounced close by us through a wood. She almost seemed to be trying to get us to notice her. When she was just out of sight behind trees, she stopped. Two of my fellow walkers claimed they saw a fawn near where she had come from, which was hidden from me behind scrubby holly trees. We decided to not investigate further for fear of causing the doe to abandon the fawn.

I caught a roe deer on camera a week later. <a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/76526364@N06/7077252297/in/photostream">www.flickr.com/photos/76526364@N06/7077252297/in/photostream</a>
