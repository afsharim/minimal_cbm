Bushy Park - July 2005 - Fallow Deer Stag - That's the One, Officer


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
<a href="http://www.royalparks.gov.uk/Bushy-Park.aspx" rel="nofollow">www.royalparks.gov.uk/Bushy-Park.aspx</a>
I have attached a link to this Royal Park's website. At the bottom right hand side of the page in very small writing is a warning notice saying that it is dangerous to approach the deer during the rutting season. It seems that the poor woman gored by an irate stag last week must have missed this particular item.
Thankfully, I don't think she was too badly injured but it pays to remember that wild animals can be exactly that when they want to be.
Anyway, get well soon and always read the small print.
