Doin' What I Do


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
I rescue bats.  The species I rescue here is <i>Eptesicus fuscus</i>, the big brown bat.  The large size rules out most Myotis species, and the keeled calcar seperates it from the little brown bat, <i>Myotis lucifugus</i>.  This one was dubbed the &quot;Christmas bat&quot;, even though it's in May.  I forget the guy's name that took this picture.  He made a big fuss about the bat being in his room though.  This probably isn't my best technique for showing the wings either.
