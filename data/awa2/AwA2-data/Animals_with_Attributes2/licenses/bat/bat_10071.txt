Christmas Bat


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
This is a big brown bat, <i>Eptesicus fuscus</i>.  They're very similar to many Myotis species, but they're larger.  They also have a keeled calcar, which the very similar <i>Myotis lucifugus</i>, the little brown bat, lacks. 

This was shot a year ago in Maryland, actually, not at Christmas.  I always have Christmas lights in my bathroom.  I got a call for a bat removal from some guy's dorm room, caught the little bugger, let her loose in my bathroom for photographing, then let her go outside.
