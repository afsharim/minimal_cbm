Pallid Bat (Antrozous pallidus) at san luis obispo botanical garden


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
this afternoon i stopped at the san luis obispo botanical gardens to see a particular blue flower.  i saw donald quintana and he showed me these wonderful creatures ... thank you donald!  this bat was also alone, tucked into an upper corner of one of the garden's straw bale buildings.  his fur looked clean and well groomed.
