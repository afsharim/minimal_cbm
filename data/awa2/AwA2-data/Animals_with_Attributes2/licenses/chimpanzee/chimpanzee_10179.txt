Chimpanzee, sometimes colloquially chimp, is the common name for the two extant species of ape in the genus Pan. The Congo River forms the boundary between the native habitat of the two species: 1) Common Chimpanzee, Pan troglodytes (West and Central Africa) 2) Bonobo, Pan paniscus (forests of the Democratic Republic of the Congo)
