|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
as my photos tell, i'm not a specialist at photographing animals. yet, i think these images are interesting sociologically. i took my son to monkey world  recently. fun, relatively effective, strange place. but i couldn't help feeling like the orientalist anthropologist when taking photographs. mostly they feel like this first one. according to current scientific thinking, here are beings that closely match us, huddled around on cold cement floors, amongst straw and bizarre S&amp;M apparatus (though they had plenty [sic] of outdoor space, should they care to swing around in little more than zero cº). 

still, the 88 little capuchins rescued in january from a s. american lab, where they were claimed to be kept entirely in isolation, some up to 20 years, lends to an understanding of how far centres such as this help improve lifestyles. 

it's complicated.
