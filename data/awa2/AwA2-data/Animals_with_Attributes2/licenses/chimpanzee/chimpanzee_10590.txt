Pensive Chimpanzee


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
A life in captivity can be fairly boring it would appear. When meandering through Copenhagen zoo, it becomes apparant the conditions many of the animals live under, are far from ideal. However it also becomes apparant how much the zoo has improved over the past years. Living in Auckland, I visited Auckland zoo repeatedly, a zoo that is incredibly nice with excellent living conditions. However Copenhagen Zoo is also an old zoo, and it is understandable how it can take time to &quot;recompose&quot; it. It is very nice indeed, to see the &quot;new&quot; elephant and polar bear enclosure, as well as numerous other areas.
