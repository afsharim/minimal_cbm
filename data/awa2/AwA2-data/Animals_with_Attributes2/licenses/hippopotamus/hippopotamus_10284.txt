HIPPOPOTAMI


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
- PHOTO TAKEN 10DEC02 - A herd of hippopotamuses swim in a muddy lake at the abandoned country home of former [drug kingpin Pablo Escobar] in central Colombia in Puerto Triunfo. The ten Nile hippos are all that remains of Escobar's private zoo.[ In his heyday in the 1980s, Escobar imported elephants, rhinoceroses, lions, giraffes and other exotic beasts to his lavish Hacienda Napoles ranch as a testament to his fabulous wealth.]
