                                                           Sad, Sad Hippo Bloat Song:  
THE LEADER OF THE BLOAT(loosely based on the c. 1964 oldie by the Shangri-La’s, “The Leader of the Pack,” <a href="http://www.oldielyrics.com/lyrics/the_shangri-las/the_leader_of_the_pack.html" rel="nofollow">www.oldielyrics.com/lyrics/the_shangri-las/the_leader_of_...</a>
Listen at <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syckLQQBShc" rel="nofollow">www.youtube.com/watch?v=syckLQQBShc</a>

[The Shangri-La's were proto-female punk.]

Female hippo A:  Is she really wallowing in the mud with him?
Female hippo B:  Well, there she is. Let's ask her.

Female hippo B:  Merrie, is that Toro's ring you're wearing?

Merrie:  Mm-hmm

Female hippo A:  Gee, it must be great wallowing with him.

Female hippo B:  Is he coming for you after sunset today?

Merrie: Uh-uh.

Female hippo A:  By the way, where'd you meet him?

Merrie:  I met him at the grassy shore.
He turned around,  looked at me-- and more.
You get the picture?

A &amp; B:  Yes, we see.

Merrie:  That's when I fell for the leader of the bloat.
 My pals were always putting him down, down,  sh-i-v-er.  
They said he came from the wrong side of the river.
  
A &amp; B:  Whatcha mean when ya say that he came from the wrong side of river?

Merrie:  They told me he was bad,
But I knew he was sad.
That's why I fell for the leader of the bloat.

One day Bossy said,  &quot;Ya gotta find someone new.&quot;
I had to tell my Toro, “We're through.”

A &amp; B:  Whatcha mean when ya say that ya better go find somebody new?

Merrie:  He stood there and asked me why,
But all I could do was cry:
I'm sorry I hurt you, the leader of the bloat.

He sort of pushed me and nosed me goodbye.
The tears were beginning to show
As he waddled away on that rainy night.
I begged him to be careful-- Of the giant reptile to beware. 
It’s the croc! Look out! Look out!

I felt so helpless.  What could I do--
Remembering all the things we'd been through.
Grazing on the grass, they all stop and stare.
I can't hide the tears, but I don't care.
I'll never forget him:  the leader of the bloat.

Toro’s gone past the crocodile’s jaw,
Into that giant’s cruel maw.
The leader of the bloat - now he's gone
The leader of the bloat - now he's gone.

   ---parodist:  moi
