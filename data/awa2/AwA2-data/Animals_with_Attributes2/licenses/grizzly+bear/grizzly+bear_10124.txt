Baby Grizzly


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Two young Grizzly bear cubs wait while their mother is fishing. These two cubs are in their first year and are still feeding on the mother's milk. On this occasion we watched mum fishing for salmon, and whilst this cub waited gave me a lovely eye contact moment.
