Brown bear encounter


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
It was an nice day so we went to a large pine forest about 40 km upstream from Ugut called Leykovskiy Bor. We found bear poo which was mostly cranberry, and went on chatting about maybe seeing that bear. My comrade then said in a silly voice - haha, there he is! And there he was, maybe 150 m away from us. He left the cranberry bog where he had been eating berries, walked up the small slope and stopped.
We did what any other retarded person would do - I reached for my bag hectically to get my tele lens, and we whistled to make him look at us (to get a better picture).
A small remark is that at least two (some say four) people were killed by bears around Ugut over the past month, and unusually brazen, agressive bears were spotted very close to the village and in Ugut itself (one was shot after scavenging in garbage containers for several days).
