Grizz


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
The grizzly bears at Grouse came out of hibernation!  This guy was about 15 feet from us. I had my face in the camera and was taking pictures.

Suddenly he charged at us stopping about 2 feet from the electric fence.  We all jumped, as did my heart from my chest to my throat.  It was obvious to me that he found this quite entertaining.  I was too busy collecting myself to take a picture of his sneer.
