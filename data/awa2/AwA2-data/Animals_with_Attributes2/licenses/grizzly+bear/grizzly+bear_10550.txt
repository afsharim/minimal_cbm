Kodiak Bear


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
I really love the colour of the fur of this Kodiak. Hair colors range from blonde to orange (typically females or bears from southern parts of the archipelago) to dark brown. Cubs often retain a white “natal ring” around their neck for the first couple years of life. The Kodiak's color is similar to that of their very close relative, the Grizzly bear. (Al Ain, UAE, July 2010)
