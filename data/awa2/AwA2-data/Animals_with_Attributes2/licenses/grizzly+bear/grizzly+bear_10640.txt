Road to Toklat


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Taking the shuttle ride to Toklat and back in Denali National Park.  Spotting a pair of Grizzly Bears busy eating all sorts of berries. They supposedly consume 20,000 berries a day!! And because these bears in Denali mostly feed on berries they are of this light brown color. All interesting facts I had no idea of till this bus ride.
