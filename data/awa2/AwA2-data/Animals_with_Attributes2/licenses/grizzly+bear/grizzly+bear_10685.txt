TNF ANAN


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
The Anan Wildlife Observatory, located 30 miles southeast of the town of Wrangell, is one of five Forest Service designated bear-viewing areas in the Tongass. Bear viewing areas offer visitors a safe place to view and photograph these iconic creatures 
in their natural habitats. Anan Creek also has the largest run of pink salmon in Southeast Alaska, which supports the high density of black and brown bears that gather there.
