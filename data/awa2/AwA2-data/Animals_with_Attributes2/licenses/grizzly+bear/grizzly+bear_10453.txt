Grizzly Bear


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Grizzly &amp; Wolf Discovery Center, West Yellowstone MT.  The Grizzly &amp; Wolf center houses bears and wolves, and some raptors, that are orphaned or created a nuisance earlier in life by frequenting inhabited areas looking for garbage.  These bears would likely not be able to survive in the wild.  The bears are from Alaska, Montana and Wyoming.
