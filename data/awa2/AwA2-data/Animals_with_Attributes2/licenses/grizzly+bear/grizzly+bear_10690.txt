Two Grizzly Bears 3


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
During the evening we watched two Grizzly Bears disputing a half eaten Bison carcass. Before we left a Wolf arrived but was unable to get any food with the two Bears.

The Bears were a long way away but were visible with the naked eye and very clear when seen with binoculars or a spotting scope (thanks to all the people who were prepared to share their scopes). I have uploaded these very poor photos as a reminder of a great experience. (I need a much bigger lens)
