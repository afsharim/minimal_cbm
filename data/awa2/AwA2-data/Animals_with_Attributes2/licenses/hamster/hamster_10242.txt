Hammy the New Year's miracle hamster


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
This lil guy was a Christmas present for my daughter. Well, on New Year's Eve, my cat got a hold of him and gave him a really nasty bite on the side. We thought Hammy wasn't going to make it. He was not looking good. We went to sleep, woke up, and this strong little fella was up and around, drinking, eating and looking as healthy as can be! So now we're referring to him as Hammy, the New Year's miracle hamster!
