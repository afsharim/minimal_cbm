Punakha - Phobjikha, yaks

The yak (<i>Bos grunniens</i> and <i>Bos mutus</i>) is a long-haired bovid found throughout the Himalaya region of south Central Asia, the Tibetan Plateau and as far north as Mongolia and Russia. Most yaks are domesticated <i>Bos grunniens</i>. There is also a small, vulnerable population of wild yaks, <i>Bos mutus</i>.
