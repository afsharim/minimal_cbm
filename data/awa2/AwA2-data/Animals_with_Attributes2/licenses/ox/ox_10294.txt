Highland cattle


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Highland cattle are a Scottish breed of cattle with long horns and long wavy coats which are coloured black, brindled, red, yellow or dun. This fine specimen was relaxing in the sun on a pasture in Logar valley, Slovenia.
