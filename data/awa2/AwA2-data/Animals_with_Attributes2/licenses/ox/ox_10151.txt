Carter's Grove Plantation near Williamsburg, VA

At the Plantation, seasonal crops that would have been grown during the eighteenth century are still grown.  In the fenced-in  orchard, rare breeds of cattle also typical of the time period on loan from Williamsburg's rare breed program are also to be seen.
