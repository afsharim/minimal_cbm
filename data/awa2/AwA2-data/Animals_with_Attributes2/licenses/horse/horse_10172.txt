Chelokee


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
If you have any doubts about whether Cherokee had a troubled trip in the Florida Derby (G1), the official Daily Racing Form chart should convince you:

&quot;Chelokee settled in good position for six furlongs, rallied along the rail on the turn, took up while trapped along the fence at the quarter pole, altered course to the outside in midstretch and rallied belatedly in the middle of the track.&quot;

Chelokee, making his first start in stakes competition, still finished third in the face of this adversity, just two-lengths behind winner Scat Daddy.

Although it was a promising entrance into the Triple Crown picture in a year in which no single colt has truly established superiority in the three-year-old division, Chelokee's Kentucky Derby (G1) hopes may be at the mercy of the graded stakes earnings list that gives preference to the top 20 earners. Chelokee's Florida Derby earnings placed him 22nd on that list.

&quot;I don't think third will be good enough to qualify on the the graded earnings,&quot; trainer Michael Matz said. &quot;He's getting better all the time. We know we're right on the bubble. [Jockey Ramon Dominguez] said he was on the best horse. We'll just have to wait and see. If we don't happen to make it into the Derby, he will be heard from again.&quot;Photo by Z
