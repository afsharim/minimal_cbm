Discreet Cat's return to racing delayed


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
DISCREET CAT
Adam Coglianese/NYRA photo

Discreet Cat will remain in Dubai while he recovers from a throat abscess and the four-year-old Forestry colt will not return to racing until the second half of the year, Godolphin Racing Manager Simon Crisford said on Wednesday.

The Grade 1 winner, whose unbeaten streak ended when he finished last of seven in the Emirates Airline Dubai World Cup (UAE-G1) on March 31 at Nad al Sheba racecourse, was due to ship to the United States this month in preparation for a start in the one-mile Metropolitan Handicap (G1) on May 28 at Belmont Park.

Crisford said plans are to ship Discreet Cat to the United States sometime in May.

&quot;He is still having treatment and making a slow recovery,&quot; Crisford said. &quot;We will have to delay his return to the United States and he will stay in Dubai until we can sort this out.

&quot;We were considering the Met Mile at the end of May, but that is out of the way now. We won't consider targets until we get him back into training.&quot;
