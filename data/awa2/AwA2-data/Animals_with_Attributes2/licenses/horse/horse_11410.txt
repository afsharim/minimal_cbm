Scat Daddy


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Scat Daddy has been retired due to a tendon injury in his right foreleg. (AI Wire Photo)

 

Scat Daddy, 18th place finisher of the Kentucky Derby and winner of the Florida Derby, has been retired due to a tendon injury in his right foreleg that was found Monday. His only work since the Kentucky Derby was on June 10th when he breezed four furlongs in 0:48 2/5 at Belmont Park.

&quot;Our vets told us he would need 90 days rest, so we would have run out of time to get him back for the major races this year,&quot; said trainer Todd Pletcher.

This follows Saturday's news that Horse of the Year, Invasor, has also been retired due to an injury he sustained during a workout. No plans to stud have been announced for Scat Daddy.
