Jockey Joe Bravo hit a milestone


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
In his career on Wednesday when he scored his 4,000th victory, while riding Alotofappeal in the seventh race at Monmouth Park. (AP Images)

 

The 35-year-old jockey known as &quot;Jersey Joe,&quot; is a third generation jockey and a native of Long Branch, N.J. At 13, the young jockey was riding Quarter Horses in Texas and then went on to start his Thoroughbred riding career in late 1988. Bravo's first recorded grade 1 win was in 1997 when he road Formal Gold to victory in the Donn Handicap.

At times things didn't look so bright for this jockey's riding career, as he has suffered some injuries along the way that could have ended his career. On July 27, 2001 Bravo suffered a broken leg in a spill at Monmouth and missed the rest of the year. The following year he sustained a broken wrist just eight days into the season and again missed the rest of the year.  Then despite breaking his back in a January spill at Gulfstream Park, he returned to riding in May and continued his dominance of New Jersey racing in 2006.

&quot;I just want to thank all the agents I've had throughout the years and all the trainers and owners who put me on such great horses,&quot; Bravo said. &quot;I'm just thankful for it all.&quot;
