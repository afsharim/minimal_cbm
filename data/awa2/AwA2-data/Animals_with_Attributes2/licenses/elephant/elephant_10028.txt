Some friends on holiday posted this photo to facebook - proof that one can find a good place to read if one really wants to.

When my daughter saw this photo she told me of a Primary School which had an &quot;Extreme Reading&quot; project. Children brought in photos of themselves reading in unusual places etc.
And I found that there's a group for it <a href="http://www.flickr.com/groups/extremereads/pool/with/9526286035/#photo_9526286035">Extreme Reads</a> but with only one member and one photograph – which I've now increased to two of each :)
