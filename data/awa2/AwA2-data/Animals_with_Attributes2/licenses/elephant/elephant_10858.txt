Hamburg - Hagenbeck: Elephants


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Although not my favorite animal by a long shot, I have to say the most impressive display at the Hagenbeck Zoo in Hamburg was the elephants.  There was a nice, big, open enclosure to see them, and outside you could hold out food to them and they would take it from your hand with their trunks.
