Desert Elephant


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
I just like how you can see that when these elephants in the desert areas come out of the river beds one truly gets a sense of how dry the landscape is - it is such an amazing thing to experience, seeing your first African Elephant deep in the Namib Desert.

If you care, the spot on the map is exactly where we saw this animal.  I see so many images shot in Namibia that are way off - and I can't blame people, the Flickr maps are really hard to use.  In Namibia, at least, you can't get right down on the satellite view.  And in the map view there is so little of Namibia, it's a joke.  

So, what I'm doing is going on to Google earth and getting it right from there.
