Too Close An Encounter


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
While photographing wild elephants in South Africa during the summer of 2009, I'd began to get complacent about how close I could get to them -- until this snorting young bull put me in my place! Its trunk could have torn my head or arm off, nonetheless what his tusks could have done. Scared me! But I think the elephant was smart enough to know that the warning would be all that's needed. Lesson learned, and with no injuries.

Near Alicedale, inside Shawari Game Reserve, Eastern Cape, South Africa
