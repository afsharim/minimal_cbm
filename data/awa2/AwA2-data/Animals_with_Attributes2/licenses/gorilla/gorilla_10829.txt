Western Lowland Gorilla (Gorilla gorilla gorilla)


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
The western lowland gorilla (Gorilla gorilla gorilla) is a subspecies of the western gorilla (Gorilla gorilla) that lives in secondary forests and lowland swamps in Central Africa. The western lowland gorilla eats plants (including bamboo), and occasionally insects and small reptiles.
