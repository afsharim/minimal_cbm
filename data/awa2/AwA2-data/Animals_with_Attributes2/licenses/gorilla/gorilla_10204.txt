Fatou the gorilla eating leaves


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Born in April 1957, Fatou is 54 years old! According to Wikipedia, gorilla lifespan in captivity is around 50 years -- the current world's oldest (Colo) was born December 1956 &amp; the longest life known for a gorilla (Jenny) was 55. Fatou seemed to peacefully enjoy the fading light of autumn.
