Red Squirrel_7674


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
I have lived in Stirling for nine years and this is the first time that I have seen a red squirrel this far south. They usually stay above the 45th parallel (Bracebridge, Ottawa, Montréal). This one is quite agressive. It actually chased away a grey squirrel three times its size, chattering all the way.

Other creatures are coming south. Polar bear dens have been found in Ontario along James Bay.
