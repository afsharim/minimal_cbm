Squirrel Vane


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Seen at Alverstone Mead. The famous 'Orange No.3'  (female), who appears on the BBC Nature page. It's interesting that, in recent months, her coat has changed from light orange to the colour of the darker orange squirrels.       

Wind blowing from the north.
