I tried to make the conditions challenging for this shot to test the calibrated Tamron 150-600mm lens; high contrast, high ISO, comparitively low shutter speed and with the main subject in shadow. As before, this is through the double glazing in my spare room. It seems to have had no trouble with tack sharp focus again. If you've the time to view it large you might see the detail better?
