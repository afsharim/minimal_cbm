+---------+
This is how he eats


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
A couple months ago I noticed one squirrel had to be very upright to eat. He would almost tip over backwards at times while he ate. More recently I have noticed he turns counter-clockwise when looking for peanuts. A week or so ago he was right outside my sliding glass door. He was frantically circling looking for peanuts. He was doing this so much that he would fall over dizzy then get up and resume circling. When he stops to look at me, he holds his head tipped to one side and his nose up in the air. I have no idea what caused this. It is hard to watch.  Just now he was eating peanuts when another squirrel came along and chased him away.  I have no idea what caused this.

The top shot is a picture of him eating a peanut, below is how the other squirrels look when they eat.
