byc65 Crazy squirrel


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Occasionally we put pieces of bread crust on our deck for whatever critters might like to nibble on it.

This particular Sunday afternoon, this squirrel came to visit at least 3 times.  He proceeded to eat all of the bread, but on the first two visits he spent most of his time carrying the pieces of bread to strategic hiding places - under the grill, on the railing, and next to individual fence posts.
