Grey Squirrel


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
The grey has virtually superceded the red in Britain after being introduced over a hundred years ago. They are intelligent, adventurous and brave, but some dislike their habit of eating nuts put out for birds. Although the red squirrel is scant in Britain, it is common on the continental mainland of Europe.
