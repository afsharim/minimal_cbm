R.I.P little Harvey - 12/02/08 - 14/06/08


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Over the last week Harvey became lazier, which we thought was just the Persian in him, but over the last few days he stopped eating, slept continually, and stopped having an interest in any of his favourite toys. Even the good ol’ hand under the bed sheet was of no interest. We rushed him to our vet,  who gave him an ultrasound confirming a lot of fluid in his abdomen. A large syringe load of bright yellow fluid was removed. There was still more in there. Our Vet then arranged to get some blood tests done as well to rule out some possibilities (all sounded pretty bad anyway). That night I did a lot of research online about “yellow fluid in kitten abdomen” and almost everything pointed to FIP “Feline infectious peritonitis”. Early results removed some of the slim possibilities ie: Liver or Kidney failure etc. 

Just under a month since making a decision for Sylvester after discovering his cancer and other ailments, we were forced to do it again. 

Harvey had such a great personality for a young kitten, and it’s heartbreaking to see him miss out on a good long life. 

He wasn’t here for long,  but he left a big hole and will be missed a lot.

Being cat free indoors will be extremely hard to get used to.
