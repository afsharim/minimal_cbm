Welcome home Kosi (Fanta Pants)


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Today our new kitten &quot;Fanta Pants&quot; was old enough to come home. As with our last kitten from JadeAngel Persians, he is very well adjusted. This shot was taken after he had been home 1 hour. 
Oscar and Fanta Pants have kept their distance from each other so far, but they are making good early progress. I know it won't be long until they are running amok together.
We named him Fanta Pants for his pedegree papers, however he will probably get a domestic name once we get to know his personality. 

Fanta Pants now has his everyday name now....Kosi (pronounced Kozzie). I wanted to name him after Justin Koschitzke of the Saints and when we saw the Kosy in his Pedegree bloodlines we were sold on the name.
