"ThunderWeasel'.  A newly discovered mammal from Asia.


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Found in the Jungles of Borneo, it is known as &quot;Balutanagan&quot; or &quot;ThunderWeasel&quot; to the Sarawak people.  Their diet consists of small birds and baby Orangutans.  They are also poisonous and can read the thoughts of their next meal.  Dangerous as all get up...
