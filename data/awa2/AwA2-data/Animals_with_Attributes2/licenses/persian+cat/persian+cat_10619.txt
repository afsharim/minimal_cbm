Sold our cute Kelso today. 

<b>I miss him already</b> :(

For <a href="http://www.flickr.com/groups/todaysrandomfact/">TRF: </a> I was barely a pet person, ever since I was a kid. But this time, it's different. I truly cared for Kelso and I realized it's so nice to have a pet :) Persian cats are sooo cute. And sweet. And sometimes, snobbish. I miss rubbing his chin and playing with him and just staying with him on random moments throughout the day. I miss coming home to a lazy cat. Today, Nick told me that pets remember if we've truly loved a them, and they've loved us back. That gives me hope and makes me smile :) We've truly loved Kelso, and he's always going to be part of our family :')
