Super mofumofu cat


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Mofumofu (もふもふ in Japanese characters) is a Japanese slang word which means fluffy. The word is almost always applied to cute animals that have a lot of fluffy hair. So mofumofu is the perfect word to describe this cat :)
By the way, this cat is the same cat of <a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/htakashi/8052105676">this lion cat</a>.
