Giraffes


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Giraffes are the tallest living terrestrial animals. They live in isolated pockets from Chad to South Africa in savannahs and open woodlands. Compared to other ruminants, giraffes have a longer lifespan - up to 25 years in the wild.
