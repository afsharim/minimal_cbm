Masai Giraffe


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Masai Giraffe at their feeding station in the Mixed African exhibit in the Al Ain zoo. The Masai Giraffe, also known as the Maasai Giraffe or Kilimanjaro Giraffe, is the largest subspecies of giraffe and the tallest land mammal. It is found in Kenya and Tanzania. The dominant male's spots tend to be darker in colour than those of other members of its herd. For pictures of the Masai Giraffe in the wild, see my Kenya Animals album. (Al Ain, UAE, Dec. 2012)
