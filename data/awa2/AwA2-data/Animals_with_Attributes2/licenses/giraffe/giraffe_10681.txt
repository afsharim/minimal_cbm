Giraffe


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
I am putting up some scans of slides for critical comment from my Flickr friends.  

I scanned a few slides I took in 1969 while traveling through East Africa returning to the US from serving two years in the US Peace Corps in Malaysia.  I had a Yashica Lynx 5000 range finder camera.  I used a Canoscan 8800F with no adjustments and did all the treatments in Lightroom 5.  There is lots of dust on all of them and I tried to eliminate the big ones.  I am not sure if this is in Kenya or Tanzania.  I was hitchhiking and I believe I was in a car with a family who were with the US embassy in Somalia.  I am not sure how many more of these I want to process.
