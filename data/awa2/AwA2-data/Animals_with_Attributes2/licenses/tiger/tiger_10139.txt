+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |

The Sumatran tiger (Panthera tigris sumatrae) is a rare tiger subspecies that inhabits the island of Sumatra. Classified as critically endangered by IUCN in 2008 as the population was estimated at 441 to 679 individuals.
