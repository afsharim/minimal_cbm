Tiger


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Took a trip to the New Forest at the start of the week and we went to Marwell Zoo. A great zoo for photographers, most of the animals are really accessible.  Also the animals are very active at this time of the year. There were lots of photographers at the zoo. I took just generic images but if you had the time you could get some great images here. The Tigers were roaming around and gave us at least half an hour to shoot them.
