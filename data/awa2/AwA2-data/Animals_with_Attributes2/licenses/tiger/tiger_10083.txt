Bengal Tiger


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Here comes Mister Stripes. A gorgeous Bengal Tiger strides majestically our way in his enclosure at the Al Ain zoo. Thanks to the dust on the glass pane being side lit by a late afternoon sun, a haze has formed, thus obscuring an otherwise fabulous view. The Bengal tiger (Panthera tigris tigris) is the most numerous tiger subspecies. Its populations have been estimated at 1,706–1,909 in India, 440 in Bangladesh, 124–229 in Nepal and 67–81 in Bhutan. Since 2010, it has been classified as an endangered species. The Bengal Tiger is the national animal of both India and Bangladesh. (Al Ain, UAE, July 2010)
