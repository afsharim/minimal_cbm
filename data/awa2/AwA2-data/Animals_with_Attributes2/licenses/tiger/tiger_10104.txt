Cool Dip


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Dominant male Tiger T3 cooling itself in a man-made water hole at Ranthambhore Tiger Reserve. As the summer peaks, the sources of water dries up and chances of spotting tigers at the few existing water holes brighten. But them be prepared to brave 47 degree C scorching sun in an open Jeep!
