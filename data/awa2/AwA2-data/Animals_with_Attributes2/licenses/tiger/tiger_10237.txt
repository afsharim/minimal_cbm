Sumatran Tiger (Panthera tigris sumatrae)


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Sumatran tiger (Panthera tigris sumatrae) is a subspecies of tiger found on the Indonesian island of Sumatra. About 400-500 wild Sumatran tigers were believed to exist in 1998, but their numbers have continued to decline.In March 2008 there were approximately 300 Sumatran Tigers remaining in the wild. The Sumatran tiger is the smallest of the tiger subspecies as compared to the Siberian tiger which is the largest.

Photographed at the Miller Park Zoo
