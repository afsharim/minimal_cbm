Sumatran Tiger


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Sumatran Tigers are highly endangered and are only found on the Island of Sumatra. 

If one wants to view tigers in the wild, they may be viewed from elephants in India's Kaziranga National Park, located  in Assam.
