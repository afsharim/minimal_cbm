Rescued Tiger


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
For reasons that escape me, there are several states that allow, in one form or another, large cats to be kept as pets. Except, of course, noone is really capable of caring for one of these guys. So on a regular basis animal control has to deal with, for example, someone's pet tiger.

<a href="http://www.turpentinecreek.org/" rel="nofollow">Turpentine Creek</a> is a USDA licensed refuge for Big Cats located in Northwestern Arkansas. Running on private donations, they care for the rescued animals.
