Charlie


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
our first dog. 

look at how fat he looks in this photo! he lost a lot of weight when he started to live with my sister.

Charlie might have been the inspiration for the Taco Bell &quot;You Quiero Taco Bell&quot; Chihuahua commercials...my sister used to live by the corporate headquarters for Taco Bell in Orange County, CA and had gone through their drive thru one evening. Charlie was in the car with her and when the person gave her the food, Charlie started to sniff the bag and started to eat a taco. The lady who was serving my sister thought that was so funny...then a year later, those commercials came out.
