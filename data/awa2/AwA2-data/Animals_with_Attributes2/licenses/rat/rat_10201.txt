Maia, our miracle rat


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Portrait of Maia.  
Maia is special because we brought her back from the brink of death.  She had a severe respiratory infection last year.  She even had a collapsed lung.  You could hear her breathe from elsewhere in the house!  At her worst, she was closing her eyes, not eating, not drinking, not doing anything!  With help from our vet, we miraculously brought her back.  She bounced back quickly, but her death rattle persisted for weeks.  The infection hung on and would not let go.  I must have taken her to the vet ten times over the course of three months.  We tried all kinds of meds, put her in the nebulizer twice a day for weeks, and saw no improvement in her breathing.  Then, after like three months of this (and a lot of cash), she cleared up seemingly overnight.  Our vet was as shocked as we were, but we're just happy to still have her around!  She's got the best temperment you could want in a pet.  A true sweetheart.
