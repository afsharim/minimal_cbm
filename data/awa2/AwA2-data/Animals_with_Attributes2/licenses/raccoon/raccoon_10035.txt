Baby Raccoon


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
In the cleft of a tree in Clear Lake State Park in California.

We watched this little guy climb way up into this live oak, turn around once, and then nap for about three hours.  Finally he came down and I captured this image before he scampered all of the way to the ground and headed for Kelsey Slough.
