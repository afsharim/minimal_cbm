The Friendly Neighbourhood Raccoon


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Well a family of them. The mother and 4 young-ins. Behold one of the young-ins.
They were quite friendly even though at some point I was 2 meters away. 
Warning though, they may contain rabies, so don`t get too close....

FYI: Raccoons are noted for their intelligence, with studies showing that they are able to remember the solution to tasks up to three years later.
