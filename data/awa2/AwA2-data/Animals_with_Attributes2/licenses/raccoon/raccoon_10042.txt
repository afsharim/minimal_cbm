BB the Birdseed Bandit eating Seeds


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
I woke up this morning and when I opened the backyard screen door I thought it was a fox back by the bird feeder between the fire pit and pool, because it had a slightly red tail and was kind of brown colored.  But then it turned and looked at me and I could see the raccoon face.  It was digging the larger nuts out of the sod and eating what the birds had knocked out on the ground.  We had thought the larger Blue Jays were doing this because we have quite a few living here.  Curious, I wanted to check the raccoon closer so I took it a plate of raw shelled sunflower seeds out on a plate.  He really liked that dish.  That is when I named him BB, the birdseed bandit.  He finally left and the little birds came back.  I will have to start watching for him closer.
