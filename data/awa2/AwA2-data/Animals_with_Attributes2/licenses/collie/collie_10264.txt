I was at the Toledo Metroparks dog festival in 08. A man was there doing a disc dog demo and this little guy was one of this crew. I can't remember the dog's name, but he was a mix of a Australian cattle dog and a rat terrier, so the trainer called him a Rattledog. He was probably about a 20 to 25 lb dog and he was jumping as high or higher than the border collies and aussies.
