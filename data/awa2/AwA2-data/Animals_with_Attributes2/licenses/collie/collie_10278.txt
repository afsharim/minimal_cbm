My current foster girl.  She is SUCH a sweetie!!!  She is available for adoption!  She is a 1 yr old AKC special needs female Smooth Collie.  She completed a basic obedience class.   She was born nearly blind but still plays and is loving life!  She has been playing with my guys like crazy.  She is amazingly adaptable!   A very affectionate and fun-loving girl.  Housetrained and very well mannered.   Up to date on vaccines, etc.
