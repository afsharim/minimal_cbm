When Paul was young his dog Bobby used to balance Brazil nuts on his nose,crack them open and swallow them down.Paul has trained our dog Stan to balance dog biscuits on his nose ( or so he told me) and we thought we would try nut balancing. After an internet search we  learnt Brazil nuts aren't toxic but could cause digestive dysfunction so dog biscuits were at hand as a reward for successfully completing the balancing trick. It took a few attempts which included&quot; I don't think I trained him as well as I should&quot; and   a biscuit pinching incident. Stan managed in the end and was rewarded with the remaining biscuits.
