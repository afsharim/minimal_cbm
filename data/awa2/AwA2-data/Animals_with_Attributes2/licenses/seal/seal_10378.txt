Galápagos Fur Seal


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
The Galapagos Fur Seal is endemic to the islands, i.e. it does not live anywhere else. Mums will spend up to 4 days hunting before returning to feed their pup. While they are away, the pup must look after themselves, and definitely avoid neighbouring females, as they can be seen as a threat and killed.
