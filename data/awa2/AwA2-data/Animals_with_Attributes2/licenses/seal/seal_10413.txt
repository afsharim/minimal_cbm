Harbor Seal (Phoca vitulina)


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
At the base of Northwestern Glacier (Kenai Fjords NAtional Park) there thousands of burgie bits (chunks of ice that have calved off of the glacier). And these chunks of ice must make it a relatively safe area for young harbor seals relative to killer whales, as I counted over 350 female seals with a young at their side...
