Private Beach


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Hearst Beach, about a half mile south of the pier.

The foot prints are intriguing....

Three big bachelor E-seals here this PM, plus countless harbor seals on the big flat rock offshore near this guy -- better seen from the bluff above (first turnout south of Hearst Beach on CA-1).

The Big Guy (currently) under the pier is a true widebody, with a most impressive &quot;trunk&quot;.
