Cookie Cutter Shark Bites


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
There is a lot of information about this shark starting with <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cookiecutter_shark" rel="nofollow">en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cookiecutter_shark</a> which tells you it is a small dogfish shark which refers to its feeding habit of gouging round plugs out of larger animals.   The female e-seals separate from the male e-seals and dive deeper and come in contact with these sharks, which are quite impressive when you look at what they do and what they look like.   The females do not always show the bites as clearly as seen in this photo.
