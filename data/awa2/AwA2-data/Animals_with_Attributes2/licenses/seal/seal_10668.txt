Ribs


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Here's another shot of the <a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/candiedwomanire/3596633/">Addams family</a>, with the pups that are all going to die. The pup at the mother's head is laying on his back and you can see his ribs. No one is going to rescue them. This is part of the Monterey Bay National Marine Sanctary and they let nature take its course unless the problem is obviously man made (e.g.  a fishing line).  Even then, it would be too dangerous for anyone to go into the middle of the colony to rescue a seal.
