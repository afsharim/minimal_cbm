2009 02 08 - 1490 - Washington DC - National Zoo - Seal


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
This seal was *not* happy... he was definitely putting on an aggressive pose in defiance of the cheers and jeers coming from the surrounding crowd.  Each time the seal would bark (and this was definitely the &quot;leave me alone&quot; sort of bark; not the &quot;yay fishies&quot; sort of bark), the crowd would erupt with delight... that seemed to anger the seal even more.  I suspect not a single person in the crowd quite realised that this was one unhappy seal.
