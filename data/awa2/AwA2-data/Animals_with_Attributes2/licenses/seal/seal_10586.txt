N. Elephant Seal (Mirounga angustirostris) - Adult bull with pup in mouth


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
The adult male N. elephant seal decided to take out his frustration on this pup that was separated/weaned from its mother.  The pup was away from the main haulout group and had no where to go.  After the adult moved on I could not see any visible damage to the pup.
