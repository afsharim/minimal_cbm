Hawaiian Monk Seal


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
I am not sure if they are endangered or just protected but I know that they are pretty rare to see.  This guy came up on the beach while we were shooting in Lahaina and they shut down the beach so that no one messed with him.  He's not dead - they do this I guess as part of their nature.
