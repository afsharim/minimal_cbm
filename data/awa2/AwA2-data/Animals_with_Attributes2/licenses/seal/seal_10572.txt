Monk Seal


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
This is a Hawaiian monk seal, a critically endangered seal native to the Hawaiian islands. It's estimated only 1100 seals are left. In 2008, it was made Hawaii's state animal.
