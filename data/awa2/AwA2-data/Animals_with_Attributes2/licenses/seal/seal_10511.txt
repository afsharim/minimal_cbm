Its A Tail!


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
See the seal lifting her tail? See how there's a little black tail below hers? That's her baby. Twenty minutes later, the pup was born.

The docent was still here at this point and she said that most seals were born head first, but the three I watched were all breech.
