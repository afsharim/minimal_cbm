Endangered Hawaiian Monk Seal


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
The Hawaiian monk seal is an endangered earless seal that is endemic to the Hawaiian Islands.

The population of Hawaiian monk seals is in decline. In 2008, it was estimated that only 1200 individuals remain.
