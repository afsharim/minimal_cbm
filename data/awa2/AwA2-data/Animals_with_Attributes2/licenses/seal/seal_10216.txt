Elephant Seal Weaners


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
They call them &quot;weaners&quot; (weiners) because these are newly weaned pups. 

Elephant seals nurse for 28 days and grow from 80 lbs at birth to nearly 300 lbs after a month of nursing.
