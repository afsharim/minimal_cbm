+---------+
No Trespassing


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Male northern elephant seal at Arroyo Laguna beach south of the official elephant seal viewing area, Piedras Blancas, CA.  Three shots using progressive front to back focal points and the CS5 features of &quot;autoalign&quot;,&quot;autoblend&quot; to increase DOF.  I may have done better to use 4 or 5 shots, but you can see this elephant seals eyes were open, he was snuffling a bit and had that &quot;offa my beach, woman&quot; demeanor to him. Time to back off. Only a little cropping here to correct for layer alignment at 400mm
