Elephant Seals


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Elephant seals, such as these seen in late October at Fortuna Bay on South Georgia Island, are at sea 10 months of the year with 90 percent of that time spent underwater. They can dive up to one kilometer deep in search of squid.
