Blue Whale (Balaenoptera musculus) Feeding on Krill off Morro Bay, CA 12 July 2010.
Subsea Tours Dos Osos Blue Whale Watching Photomorrobay Tour 12 July 2010, 9AM - Noon. (This was the 4th of 5 planned trips). 18 Photomorrobay members go out (seven miles west of Morro Rock) in search of Blue Whales (Balaenoptera musculus) and we had one extended sighting.
