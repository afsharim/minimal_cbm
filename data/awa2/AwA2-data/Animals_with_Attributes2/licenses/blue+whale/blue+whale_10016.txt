Mike Bairds Dos Osos Whale Trips were successful as we did see some Blue Whales (Balaenoptera musculus) We shut off the motors so that we could hear them better.   What a treat to both see and hear the whales, even though they were out there a little distance.  Too bad we could not have gotten closer, but the captain did not want to spook them as they can swim faster than our boat.
