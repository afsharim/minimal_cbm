Masai/ Plains Zebra


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
A group of Masai Zebra settles down to a late lunch. Pity these creatures hae to spend all their lives in a sand pit. In the wild, they live in more lush surroundings. The plains zebra, also known as the common zebra or Burchell's zebra, is the most common and geographically widespread species of zebra. It ranges from the south of Ethiopia through East Africa to as far south as Botswana and eastern South Africa. For pictures of the Masai Zebra in the wild, see my Kenya Animals set. (Al Ain, UAE, Dec. 2012)
