Racine Zebra


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
On our way up to Door County, Eden, Nana and Jim stopped at the Racine Zoo, a sweet little collection of animals.  This zebra works the afternoon shift in this enclosure, which is used by rhinos in the morning.

This is a Damara zebra, also known as Chapman's zebra, a subspecies of the plains zebra.  Note the distinctive pale &quot;shadow stripe&quot; between the darker stripes on the rump.
