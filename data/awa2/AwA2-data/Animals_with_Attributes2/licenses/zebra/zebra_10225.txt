grevys zebra - San Diego Zoo


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Grévy's zebra at the San Diego Zoo in San Diego, California.

Grévy's zebra (also known as the Imperial Zebra) is the largest zebra -- being 8 to 10 from head to tail and 4'7&quot; to 5'3&quot; high at the shoulder.  These zebras weigh 770 to 990 pounds. Grévy's zebra has a more mule-like appearance, with a large head, narrow nostrils, round ears, and short and thick neck.

Grévy's zebra is found in Kenya and Ethiopia, but is extremely endangered. Just 3,000 live in the wild.
