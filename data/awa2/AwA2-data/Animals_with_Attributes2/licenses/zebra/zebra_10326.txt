Masai Zebra


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
A herd of Masai Zebra in the Masai Mara. There are several of them all over the place and you see them fairlyfequently in large groups. Family ties are very strong among zebras. Several are killed and eaten by lions, and several are also hit by fast moving vehicles at night as they cross busy highways that cut through some of the wildlife areas. (Oct/ Nov. 2010)
