Canada lynx


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Canana lynx (Lynx canadenis) is a medium-sized cat with long legs, large, well-furred paws, long tufts on the ears, and a short, black-tipped tail.  The Canada lynx is listed as threatened.
