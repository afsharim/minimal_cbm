Mama Bobcat - A Series


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
In Montana de Oro State Park I spied a young bobcat who led me to its beautiful mother (above).  They had a few 'mews' together then went their separate ways.  Here the Mom takes off and I quickly lost track of her as she went under the Cypress Trees.  Eventually the young one came out again and I watched as it hunted, caught and then ate a gopher.  The series of images can be seen below.  A word of caution to the more delicate viewers...a few of the shots might be considered gory (I left the worst of them un-posted).
I'm pretty sure this is the same Bobcat I captured in July... <a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/goingslo/4821812874/">www.flickr.com/photos/goingslo/4821812874/</a>
