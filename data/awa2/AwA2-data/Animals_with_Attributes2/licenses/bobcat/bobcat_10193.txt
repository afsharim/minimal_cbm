Bobcat (Lynx rufus)


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
The bobcat (Lynx rufus) is a North American mammal of the cat family. The bobcat resembles other species of the Lynx genus but is on average the smallest of the four. Its coat is variable, though generally tan to grayish brown, with black streaks on the body and dark bars on the forelegs and tail. Its spotted patterning acts as camouflage. The ears are black-tipped and pointed, with short black tufts. There is generally an off-white color on the lips, chin, and underparts. 

Photographed at Miller Park Zoo
