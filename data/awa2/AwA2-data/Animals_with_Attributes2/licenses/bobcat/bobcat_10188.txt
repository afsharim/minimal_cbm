Bobcat - Lynx rufus


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Montana de Oro State Park in front of the Spooner Ranch House.  Few people were in the park.  Weather was cool, breezy and cloudy.  This smallish bobcat was first seen sitting by a gopher mound.  When a car drove up he/she abandoned its vigil and strolled across the road and into the brush.
