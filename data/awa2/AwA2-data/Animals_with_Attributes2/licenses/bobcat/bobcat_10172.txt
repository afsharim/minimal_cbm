Watchable Wildlife in Baker County Oregon 
Baker County’s diverse geography and habitat are ideal for wildlife watching.
In addition to great year round birding, visitors can easily see deer, bighorn sheep, elk, and antelope, along the three scenic byways that connect in Baker City.  Less common but just as exciting are the occasional sightings of animals like this Bob Cat spotted in the Burnt River Canyon near the town of Durkee
 Birding enthusiasts can see more than 300 bird species throughout Baker County.  
A few of our favorite birding hot spots include the Sumpter Dredge Wildlife area between McEwen Station and the Sumpter Dredge <a href="http://www.oregonstateparks.org/park_239.php" rel="nofollow">www.oregonstateparks.org/park_239.php</a>, and along the Hells Canyon Scenic Byway <a href="http://www.idahopower.com/pdfs/ourEnvironment/hcBirdCheckList.pdf" rel="nofollow">www.idahopower.com/pdfs/ourEnvironment/hcBirdCheckList.pdf</a>, 
For more information about birding and other watchable wildlife opportunities in Baker County and to download your Baker County Bird Checklist visit the Baker County Tourism website at <a href="http://www.basecampbaker.com/bird-watching-in-baker-county-oregon.html" rel="nofollow">www.basecampbaker.com/bird-watching-in-baker-county-orego...</a>
