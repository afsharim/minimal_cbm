Bobcat


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Bobcats are nocturnal, they're mostly active at night. It is rare to see them during the day. They are solitary hunters, able to leap up to 10 feet to catch their prey. Their diet consists mostly of small animals such as rabbits, mice, birds and squirrels.
