Orca i43 (i11 Pod)


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
We got lucky and saw lots of critters on our whale-watching ride. Along with porpoises, dolphins, and jumping salmon, we also saw a pod of Orca whales.  Our guide identified the pod by the white markings behind the dorsal fins of some of its members.  This would have been a good occasion for a lens longer then 105mm..
