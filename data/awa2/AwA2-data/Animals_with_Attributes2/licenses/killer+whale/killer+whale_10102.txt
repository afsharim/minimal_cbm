Killer Whale (Resident Orca)


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
I had the opportunity to photograph and be around Orca Whales this past weekend on the west coast. I witnessed both resident and transient orcas. They are truly remarkable creatures. With the engines of the boat turned off and the whales passing the boat hearing them blow water is a very peaceful and tranquil experience. Their presence and size is breathtaking.
