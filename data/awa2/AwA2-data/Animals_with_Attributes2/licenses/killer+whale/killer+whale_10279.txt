Surfaced Orca II


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
This photo has it all:  the distinctive, immensely tall vertical dorsal fin of a male Orca; the diagnostic gray spotch on the body at the base of the fin; the blow hole; and, best of all, <em>Orca breath</em>!

Now, in my whale book, the Orcas are spouting with enough force to knock a person off his feet.  This guy was breathing in dainty puffs.  

You don't want to know what I had to do to make the exhalation more visible in this photo.  But I assure you no cutting and pasting was involved.  I tried something with Photo Shop CS4 that made the exhalation look like the proverbial mushroom-shaped cloud.  Not saved.

Elapsed time between &quot;Surfaced Orca I&quot;  and &quot;Surfaced Orca II&quot;: 32 seconds.

At sea, on the Strait of Georgia, between Nanaimo and Comox, on the eastern shore of Vancouver Island, British Columbia, Canada.
