Orca


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
These are from the San Juan Orcas, in a way these are vegetarian Orcas as they eat only salmon and do not hunt other sea mammals like dolphin, whales, sea lions etc. Other Orcas do that which is why they are often called Killer Whales. They aren't even whales they belong to the Dolphin family. The Orcas we saw were the resident Orcas from J-Pod. Among them one called Granny  who is 101 years old (as estimated) and a mom and her calf called Cookie (mom) and Oreo (child)
