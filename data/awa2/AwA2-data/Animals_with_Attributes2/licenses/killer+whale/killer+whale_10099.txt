<b>#389</b> - Killer whale at Sea World in San Diego, California. The killer whale or orca (<i>Orcinus orca</i>), less commonly, blackfish or seawolf, is the largest species of the Dolphin family. It is found in all the world's oceans, from the frigid Arctic and Antarctic regions to warm, tropical seas.
