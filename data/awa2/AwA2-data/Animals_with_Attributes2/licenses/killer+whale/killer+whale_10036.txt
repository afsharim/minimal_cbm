Bjossa the Orca


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
This is Bjossa, the orca (or killer whale) that lived at the Vancouver Aquarium for many years.  This photo was taken in September 1999, shortly before she was moved to San Diego in 2000.  Sadly, she died in 2001. <a href="http://www.captiveanimals.org/aquarium/bjossa2.htm" rel="nofollow">www.captiveanimals.org/aquarium/bjossa2.htm</a>
