Cow


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
I'm finding that getting a good cow picture is much harder than it looks. First it's the ear tag thing. Most cows around here have big bright colored ear tags (what's with that). While on vacation I found some without tags, so after passing them up I turn around and go back, always a car right behind me at this point, so finding a safe turnaround point is not so easy.  All excited I have to shoot some photos.  My wife is very patient with me even encouraging me. But I find myself stopped on the side of a small busy narrow road, flashers on so everyone can see the nut out with his camera shooting cow pictures.  I'm sure the local farmers get a kick out of that one. Memories of my soybean shot come to mind. Standing just off the road in a soy field a farmer pulls up and stops his car calls out to me &quot;aint you ever seen a soybean field before?&quot; Now try to explain what a great shot this will make. Show him the shot, he is not impressed. Maybe he has seen my work. The next thing that happens with cow pictures is the way they group themselves in the field. Composing the photo, this group, that group. too far away, cows part way behind the other cows. very unorganized.  I shoot quickly so I can get out of there. That is my next mistake.The results, lots of cows in photos. Nothing great. Why do the photos look better on the camera back than they do on the PC?  So the quest for a good cow shot continues.
