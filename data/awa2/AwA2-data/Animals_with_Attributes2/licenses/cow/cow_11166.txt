Poplar Spring Animal Sanctuary


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Poplar Spring Animal Sanctuary provides a second chance for animals who often get a rough start. Abused, abandoned, and destined for a short and painful life, the universe intercedes on behalf of these fortunate few animals who receive physical care, love, and an opportunity to live out their lives in what must seem like Eden ... or just home.
