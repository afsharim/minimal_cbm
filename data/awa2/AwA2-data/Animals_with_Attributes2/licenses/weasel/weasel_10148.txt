Weasel


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Went for a walk with Cuan today and saw this little guy just down the road.  I've never actually seen a weasel before this, only stoats - and never this close to home.

After popping his head out a few times he made a <a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/threefingers/2382794224/">swim for it</a> across the stream and scaled the hillside.  Twas pretty cool.

North Least Weasel - <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Least_Weasel" rel="nofollow"><i>Mustela nivalis nivalis</i></a>
