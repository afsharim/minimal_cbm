Weasel


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
I had the wonderful experience this lunchtime (<i>11-Dec-2015</i>) of watching a weasel for about half an hour diving under leafs and leaping over logs foraging for a meal in a Kentish woodland. 
Where in the past I have evaded capturing one of these small rapid mammals in the wild, with the lens, this little chap, though wary of my presence, accommodated me by allowing its' photograph to be taken. 
I'm still smiling now as I type this message... I love nature and what it shares with you... moments like that are priceless, as I am sure you will agree.
