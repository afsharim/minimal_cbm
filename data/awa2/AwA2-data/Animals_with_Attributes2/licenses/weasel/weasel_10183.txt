Oriel


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Oriel is a female Polecat at the British Wildlife Centre.  She looks so tiny next to her mate that many people think she's a baby.  She stands up on her back feet when it's near feeding time and I heard her called a Meerkat many times today.  I think the BWC's aim to educate children about their own country's wildlife is a good one.  Education is clearly needed!
