Long-tailed Weasel


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
This long-tailed weasel has been a frequent visior at the headquarters of Arapaho NWR. This series of photos was taken May 10, 2013 while the weasel ran from hole to hole searching for ground squirrels and prairie dogs.
