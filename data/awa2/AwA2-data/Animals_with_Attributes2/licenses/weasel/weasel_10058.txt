Himalayan Zoo, Darjeeling


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
A weasel amuses the onlookers by climbing vertically up his cage. Though appearing funny, it's probably tragic, as it probably misses  the trees outside. Weasels are small, active predators, long and slender with short legs.  They feed on small mammals, and have from time to time been considered vermin, since some species took poultry from farms, or rabbits from commercial warrens. They occur all across the world except for Antarctica, Australia, and neighbouring islands. (May 2009)
