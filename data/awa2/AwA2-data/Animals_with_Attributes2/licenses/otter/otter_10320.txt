Otter


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
An otter sighted near Westing, Unst.  First saw him swimming underwater, hugging the bottom, in the crystal clear seas off Shetland.  He reached these rocks, climbed up to look back and, a few seconds later, swam off out of sight.
