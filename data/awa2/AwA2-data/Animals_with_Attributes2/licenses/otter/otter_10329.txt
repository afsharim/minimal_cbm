Otter


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
One of the most beautiful creatures on the island of Britain, yet one of those routinely singled out as a nuisance. The otter is amongst my favourite wild animals in the UK and we were lucky enough to see them being fed recently on a trip to Drusillas zoo park.

I snapped a lot of this cute little fella, but alas, they all seemed rather boring, so out came the HDR kit for a bit of a play. When it comes to landscapes, I am now fairly comfortable with HDR but wildlife presents a whole new set of problems that I am yet to solve. 

Still, every flickr account should have at least one photograph of an otter in it ;)
