Big Bull Moose


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
This big bull moose was on the trail heading to the Feldtman Tower from Siskiwit Bay Campground.  We were just doing the normal thing of always looking down in front of you about 2 to 3 feet watching for roots and rocks when we ran right into him in the middle of the trail. We stood and watched him for about 15 minutes before he just walk off into the woods.
