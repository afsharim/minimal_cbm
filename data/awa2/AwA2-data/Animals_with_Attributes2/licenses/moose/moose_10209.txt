Momma Moose


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
So, this weekend I discovered it's possible to drive Fort St John and back in 36 hours (total kilometers driven: 2600; short version of the reason: my parents hit a deer on their way to collect my sister and her belongings from Fort Nelson and totaled their truck; my brother and I drove up to deliver a rental vehicle as this was cheaper then renting a vehicle up there.). There wasn't a lot of opportunity for photography, although I saw enough to convince me that I need to come this way again when I'm not in a rush. 

On the way back, we spotted a moose and a baby moose, which you can just make out in the center of this photo.
