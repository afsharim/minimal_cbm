Pig Racing


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
The winner, approaching the finish, is The 'Middle White' on the right. It's face reveals how this variety resulted from cross breeding with asian pigs. The ginger-coloured Tamworth Pig, coming in third, won the first race. Tamworths are an 'unimproved' species, being closest to their wild boar siblings. They are hardy, inquisitive and fast moving. Two escaped from a UK abattoir a few years ago and evaded captures for ages.
