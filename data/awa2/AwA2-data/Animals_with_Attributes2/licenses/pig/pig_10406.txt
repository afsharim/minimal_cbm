Pigs at Fadiouth (Fadiout), Sénégal (West Africa) 1982


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Photo taken on January 17, 1982.  Pigs are not a usual sight in most of Sénégal since most of the population is Muslim.  The Sérèr people, though, are not and thus have almost a monopoly on pig-raising in this part of the country.  The pigs are mostly sold (for example in Dakar) instead of being eaten by the Sérèr and many of them are exported.  For more on the remarkable and complex organic farming system of these people, see LES PAYSANS DU SENEGAL by Paul Pélissier, pages 183-299.  Pigs are discussed on page 260, where he writes, &quot;Il y a là pour les Sérèr, une source de profit d'autant plus assurée qu'ils sont certains d'en garder l'exclusivité en raison de l'adhésion unanime à l'islam de toutes les autres populations rurales habitant au Nord de la Gambie.&quot;
