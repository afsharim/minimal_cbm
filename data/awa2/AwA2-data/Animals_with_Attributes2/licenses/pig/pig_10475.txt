Pointer Ruby


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Ruby is a Gloucestershire Old Spots pig who was rescued from a &quot;pastured pork&quot; operation. Even though the pigs get to live outside, runty piglets are still killed. A visitor to the farm offered to take the piglet off the farm's hands and they agreed. She nursed little Ruby back to health and now Ruby lives at Animal Place. She will be spayed and then introduced to the other pigs. She is one cute piglet!!
