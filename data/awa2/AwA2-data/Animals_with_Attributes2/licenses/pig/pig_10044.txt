Alisha's FFA Pig,,,,,, Bullwinkle


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Well,,,,, I have become a <b>Super Mom</b> in the past 8 weeks helping my daughter become more responsible &amp; grow &amp; learn about the care in Animals, specifically Pigs.
This has been a VERY trying &amp; my daughter learning appreciation in so many different ways during this experience has been mentally &amp; physically exhausting.  
This is a shot I took 11 days ago on my phone, we are in a crunch for this specific pig to make weight for our local fair, (Ingham County Fair).  A pig to go to the fair has to be <b>#220</b> or it can not enter the fairgrounds to show, well this particular pig has been weighted at #180 at this picture &amp; I had to come up with someway for Bullwinkle to make #40 in 12 days.  After calling &amp; meeting with a local pig farmer, he did not think that Bullwinkle would ever get to fair weight.  I then got onto the internet &amp; researched with the help of Gollygforce (G) &amp; found a few other ways to get him to eat more &amp; FAST!  
I had to buy mass amounts of Eggs, Molasses, Dry Milk, Corn Oil &amp; Ground Corn.  This mixture made into an oatmeal like mash has gained <b>#22</b> pounds in 5 days, but my daughter &amp; I have had to hand feeding him <b>4X</b> a day.  I also have had a <u>Swine Professor from Michigan State University</u> come to the barn to see if there was anything else we could do to get Bullwinkle up to weight Faster!  He was a great help &amp; stated we were doing already everything we could do for the length of time we had left.  
For those that do not know a the background on me,,, I have 3 herniated disks in my neck &amp; I blew out my right knee in March &amp; have a Surgery on my knee to have a Lateral Release &amp; Meniscus repair September 8th 2011.  I am a walking basket of pain &amp; this pig <b>S T I N K S !!!!!!!</b>  Every time I come home from the barn (90-100 degree days) for the past 2 weeks, I smell so bad that I have to change in my Garage before I enter my own house!  I grew up on a farm but not with Pigs!  Whew,,,,, wanted to share my past MANY weeks of preparation for the fair for my daughter to have a<u> good time, Learn, Grow, Appreciate</u> &amp; just have some fun in FFA Swine.
Fair starts August 1-6 &amp; I just hope all our <b>HARD WORK</b> will show Alisha a different <u>respect</u> for animals &amp;<u> ME</u>!
She will be selling Bullwinkle the day after showing at the Fair auction.  
Pray for Alisha that her pig makes weight to get into the fair. 
Pray for Alisha to get Bullwinkle sold in auction or I have to figure that one out after the fair &amp; my freezer can't hold all that meat!
And most of all,,,,<b>PLEASE</b> Pray for me, my sanity &amp; my health for I am utterly exhausted Mentally &amp; Physically!
