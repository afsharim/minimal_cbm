Marigold!


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Marigold is a 6-mos-old piglet who discovered the big pig's pig pond! She escaped from a backyard butcher and was found loose on the streets of Sacramento where city animal control caught her. 

She is at the age and weight that pigs in the US are needlessly killed. Marigold wants to live, play in the mud, make friends, and enjoy her life.

She gets to do that at Animal Place.
