Putting Lipstick On A Pig: The Show's Over, Or Is It?


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
It's a wrap! Mrs. von Liski's second attempt to kiss the pig goes off without a hitch, and even leaves Cupcake with a sweet smile. Everyone though the show was over as students filed back into their classrooms, but Cupcake had one more trick up his sleeve. The adventurous pig decided to lead his owners on a block long chase after being let down and escaping his harness. Fortunately, he was recovered safely and was last seen being driven away by Ms. St. John. He gave us a little wink as he left the area.
