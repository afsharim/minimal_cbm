|  DESCRIPTION  |

Deep bedded hoop houses provide pigs the opportunity to exhibit natural behaviors and interact with other pigs.  Straw is used for bedding, which provides nice bedding and serves to reduce odors and eliminate a need for manure pits or manure lagoons.
