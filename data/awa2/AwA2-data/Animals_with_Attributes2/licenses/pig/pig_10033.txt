3 Little Pigs


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
Three piglets share a feeding trough in their cement pen on a small farm in Baliuag, Bulacan, Philippines.  The farmer bought 7 piglets hoping to have them ready for market to sell to politicians in May 2016 who use the meat to reward their staff and buy votes from citizens.
