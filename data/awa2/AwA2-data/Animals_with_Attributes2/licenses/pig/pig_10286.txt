Pig on the Umu.
So the &quot;Umu&quot; is the Samoan oven which is a fire with lava rocks, and food is put on to it to cook it. The food is then covered with banana leaves and it takes about an hour to cook a pig this size. Also in this Umu, is taro and breadfruit. We also cooked lobster and Palusami for this feast
