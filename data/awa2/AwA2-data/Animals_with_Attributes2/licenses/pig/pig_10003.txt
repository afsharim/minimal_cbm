'I like pigs' direct mail appeal from RSPCA (February 2009)


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
RSPCA's direct mail campaign aims to improve welfare standards for pigs in Europe. 

It is based on the quote from Sir Winston Churchill: &quot;I like pigs. Dogs look up to us. Cats look down on us. Pigs treat us as equals.&quot;
