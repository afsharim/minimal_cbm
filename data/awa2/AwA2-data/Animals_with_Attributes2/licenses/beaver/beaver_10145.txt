Urban Watcher


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
A woodchuck, <i>Marmota monax</i>, (they're also known as groundhogs) pauses during an evening snack to make sure I don't get too close.  They're all over Fort Meade, Maryland, like squirrels in Central Park or something.  Speaking of which, woodchucks are actually a type of large ground squirrel.  They're in the squirrel family just like every other squirrel you've ever seen; Sciuridae.
