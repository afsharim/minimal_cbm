Eurasian Beaver (Castor fiber)


+---------------+
|  DESCRIPTION  |
+---------------+
In Sweden we have 25 provincial animal and the Eurasian beaver is provincial animal in: Ångermanland

The Eurasian Beaver is little bit smaller then the Amerikan one.
Thes are Nocturnal and i dident see them befor it were to dark the evening befor, but this morning i had more luck.

Beaver are as its calld a keystone species in the ecosystem, were this exist the biodiversity offen is high.

Found this one eating bark from a branch, it whent down in the watter when i came. But surprized me when it came up agen with a nother branch! Ferst time i have seen Beaver on land. Dident get the best shot with it on land tought...

Here the Beaver lumber slowly down in to the watter. 
Their eye sight isent the best but their sens of smell is ALOT better, the hearing should not be that bad either of what i have heard.

For this specie conservation have been a real success! Here in Sweden we have them in great number agen.
