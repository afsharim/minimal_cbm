This little guy totally didn't see me until a few minutes after this shot when I tried to sneak around front.  

Apparently, an increase in beaver on the Yellowstone River is an unintended consequence of wolf reintroduction.  It seems that elk no longer graze unsafe narrow areas, allowing willow and cottonwood shoots to come up.
